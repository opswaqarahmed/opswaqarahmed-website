### Why the repositioning
"Lead Operations" described the discipline but hid the most valuable thing you build: AI that answers and acts.
Buyers now search for and budget for "AI automation" and "AI agents". The new name puts that category first,
while the bank-operations story becomes the reason to trust your AI: agents with limits, records and a human in charge.
GoHighLevel and n8n stay in every headline and service, so platform discovery is not lost.

### Personal brand vs. business brand
- **Personal brand:** Waqar Ahmed, AI Automation & Agentic AI Specialist. Used on LinkedIn, X, YouTube, GitHub and the About page.
- **Business brand (now):** Waqar Ahmed | AI Automation & Agentic AI. Same name, so trust built on the person transfers to the business.
- **Agency (later):** when a team is in place, keep "Waqar Ahmed" as the founder line and add an agency name above it. Do not create a separate name until there is a team to stand behind it.

## 2. Positioning

**One line:** AI agents and automation for renovation and home-service businesses: a 24/7 AI website receptionist,
GoHighLevel and n8n systems and conversion websites, built with the controls of bank operations.

**Who it is for:** owners and office managers of renovation, remodeling and home-service businesses with steady
enquiries that go unanswered after hours or stall between first contact, site visit and quote.

**What you sell (outcomes, then systems):**
1. Every website visitor gets an answer, day or night: **24/7 AI Website Receptionist** (RAG agent + chat widget).
2. Every enquiry gets an owner, a next step and a follow-up: **GoHighLevel lead systems**.
3. The work between tools gets done without copy-paste: **n8n workflows and AI agents**.
4. The website turns visits into enquiries: **websites built to convert** (AI-assisted builds).
5. It keeps working after launch: **automation care**.

**Why premium, without claiming it:** a narrow niche, a written scope before any build, AI with written limits,
client-owned accounts, documented handover and evidence that says exactly how it was produced.
Do not call yourself "top", "best", "leading", "#1" or "expert". The specifics do that work.

### Message order (every profile, proposal and page)
1. Who: renovation and home-service businesses.
2. Problem: visitors go unanswered after hours, and leads stall between handoffs.
3. Outcome: every enquiry answered, followed up and accounted for.
4. Method: an AI receptionist answers from your own content; GoHighLevel runs the CRM; n8n connects the rest.
5. Trust: bank-operations controls on AI, accounts you own, labelled evidence.
6. Next step: **Request an automation audit** (15 minutes, one workflow, you keep the map).

### The five controls (bank branch to AI and lead systems)
| In a bank branch | In your AI and lead systems |
| --- | --- |
| Every entry has an owner and a status. | Every lead, and every action an AI agent takes, has an owner, a status and a record. |
| Nothing is posted without authority. | AI answers come from your approved content. Prices, promises and decisions stay with your team. |
| Exceptions are dealt with, not hidden. | Every workflow has an error branch. When a step fails or the AI is unsure, a person is told. |
| Access is given by role. | Team-member invite with only the access needed. Never a password. |
| The record has to explain itself. | CRM notes, written documentation and a Loom walkthrough. |

## 3. Voice

Plain, specific, calm. Short sentences. Problem before tools. Sentence case for text; kickers may be uppercase labels.

| Write | Don't write |
| --- | --- |
| An AI receptionist answers from your own approved content, day or night. | Revolutionary AI that never sleeps! |
| Prices and promises stay with your team. | Our AI closes deals for you. |
| In one sample run, Gmail accepted four emails. Delivery was not confirmed. | Guaranteed delivery to every supplier. |
| Sandbox build for a practice account, using test data. | Client project (when it was not). |

- One call to action, worded the same everywhere: **Request an automation audit**. The form button is **Send audit request**.
- Explain AI terms once in plain words: "retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): the AI answers from your own content."
- No emoji, no arrows on buttons, no hype words (revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, supercharge).
- Words to prefer: answered, grounded, handoff, owner, next step, accounted for, record, approved content, human checkpoint.

### Evidence labels (always one of these)
- **Tested**: seen in a recorded sample run. Teal dot.
- **Estimate**: worked out from a stated assumption. Amber dot.
- **Sandbox build**: built in a practice account with test or demo data. Amber dot.
- **Build spec**: a fact about how it is built. Grey dot.
- **Illustrative example**: a diagram or sample conversation, not a recording. Say so next to it.

## 4. Visual identity

**Concept:** the agent ledger. A dark, quietly glowing "night shift" stage (the hours when the AI receptionist works)
with light, ruled sheets on top: the agent log, evidence tables, forms. The signature element is the
**Agent log** in the home hero: one after-hours enquiry, posted line by line from chat to owner call-back.

### Colour
| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night | #070F1C | Hero gradient end, flagship section, footer, header (92% with blur) |
| Deep navy | #0A1626 | Dark sections, page heroes |
| Ledger navy | #0F223A | Strong rules, primary button on light |
| Paper | #F4F6F8 | Page background (cool grey) |
| Sheet | #FCFDFD | Tables, forms, the agent log |
| Ink | #122033 | Text on light |
| Slate | #44556A | Secondary text on light |
| Teal | #0B7568 | Links, ticks, kickers and "tested" on light |
| Mint (agent signal) | #3DD9C1 | Primary button, glows and accents on dark only |
| Signal blue | #8FB4FF | Second glow colour and the end of the headline gradient, on dark only |
| Amber | #A3520B (light) / #F3B95A (dark) | Status only: estimate, sandbox, open item |

Mint and signal blue never carry text on a light background. Amber is never decoration. All text pairs meet WCAG AA.
Glows are soft radial gradients at 10 to 16% opacity, never neon.

### Typography
- **Manrope** (700 to 750) for headlines, the brand name, figures and the agent log title. Modern and precise.
- **DM Sans** for body text, labels and tables (tabular figures in tables).
- Uppercase kickers in DM Sans 700, letter-spacing 0.08em, above section headlines.
- Both fonts are open source (SIL OFL) and self-hosted in `public/assets/fonts/`. Lora is retired.

### Logo
The WA mark (unchanged): a navy tile, a white "W" and a mint full stop: the entry is posted, the lead moved forward.
Files: `logo/wa-mark.svg`, `logo/wa-mark-512.png`. Clear space of half the tile width. Do not recolour, stretch or add effects.
Lockup: mark + "Waqar Ahmed" in Manrope 750 + "AI automation & agentic AI" in DM Sans beneath.

### Imagery
Real workflow steps, agent logs, chat patterns and (once supplied) sanitised screenshots. No stock photos, robots,
glowing brains or circuit-board art. A real headshot is the one photo the brand needs.

### Layout and components
- Dark hero with a faint grid and two soft glows, then alternating Paper and white sections, with dark sections for the flagship and the trust story.
- Pill buttons. 16px corners on the agent log, chat panel and ownership block; square tables.
- Lists are ruled rows, not card grids. The flagship case is marked with a teal edge and its service name.
- Motion: entries posting in the agent log and a pulsing live dot. Both off when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
