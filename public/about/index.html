What banking taught me about AI agents.

In a branch, an entry with no owner is a problem someone has to find before the day closes. A lead with no owner is the same problem, and so is an AI agent with no limits and no record. Both cost you quietly.

In a bank branchIn your AI and lead systems
  • In a bank branch: Every entry has an owner and a status.In your AI and lead systems: Every lead, and every action an AI agent takes, has an owner, a status and a record.
  • In a bank branch: Nothing is posted without authority.In your AI and lead systems: AI answers come from your approved content. Prices, promises and decisions stay with your team.
  • In a bank branch: Exceptions are dealt with, not hidden.In your AI and lead systems: Every workflow has an error branch. When a step fails or the AI is unsure, a person is told.
  • In a bank branch: Access is given by role.In your AI and lead systems: I work through a team-member invite with only the access the work needs. Never your password.
  • In a bank branch: The record has to explain itself.In your AI and lead systems: CRM notes, written documentation and a Loom walkthrough, so your team can run it without me.
