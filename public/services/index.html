Services

AI where it helps. Automation where it pays. A person where it matters.

No one-size-fits-all AI pitch. We map your process, agree an outcome in writing, then build and test the smallest system that fixes it: an AI receptionist, a GoHighLevel and n8n workflow, a website, or all of them working together. Delivered remotely, inside accounts you own.

    • Clear statuses and useful alerts
    • Knowledge base updates for the AI receptionist
    • Handover notes your team can follow

How a build runs

AI and automation should make work easier to see and easier to recover. Every project has agreed tests, a human owner and a fallback.

  1. Audit

    A 15-minute call. We trace one workflow and find where leads stall and where AI would actually help.

  2. Map and scope

    We agree the outcome, what is included, third-party and AI usage costs, and turnaround in writing before any build.

  3. Build and test

    Built inside your accounts with test records and test questions first: duplicates, missing data, failed actions and questions the AI should not answer.

  4. Hand over

    Documentation and a Loom walkthrough. You own every workflow, agent, knowledge base, login and record.

Who this is for, and who it isn't

A good fit

  • Renovation, remodeling and home-service businesses with steady enquiries that stall between first contact, site visit and quote.
  • Owners who want AI to answer and qualify enquiries, with a person still in charge of prices and promises.
  • Teams already on GoHighLevel, or ready to run their own GoHighLevel subscription.
  • Businesses that want documented systems their own team can run.

Not a fit

  • Anyone looking for an ad agency or bought leads. I build the systems, not the traffic.

Questions owners ask first

What is the 24/7 AI Website Receptionist?

An AI agent with a chat widget on your website. It answers visitor questions from a knowledge base built from your own approved content (this is called retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG), and captures the enquiry so a person on your team can follow up.

Will the AI make things up or quote prices?

It is set up to answer from your approved content, and to hand over to a person when the answer is not there. Prices, commitments and anything that needs judgement stay with your team. We agree these rules in the scope and test them with real questions before launch.

What does "agentic AI" mean for my business?

An AI agent does more than chat: it can take steps, such as looking something up, filling in a CRM record or starting a follow-up. I build those steps inside workflows with logs, limits and a human checkpoint, so you can see and approve what the agent does.

Do I need a GoHighLevel account?

For CRM work, yes. I build inside your GoHighLevel account, so you own the workflows, contacts and data. The AI receptionist and website can also connect to the tools you already use.

Will you need my password?

No. I ask for a team-member invite with the access the work needs, never your password.

When do you use n8n instead of GoHighLevel alone?

GoHighLevel runs the CRM process: forms, pipelines, calendars, follow-up and tasks. I add n8n when the work needs integration, data handling, AI agent steps or exception paths that GoHighLevel can't do well alone.

What does it cost?

The build fee is quoted in writing after the audit, before any work starts. Running costs depend on your own plans, such as GoHighLevel, n8n hosting, AI model usage and any email or SMS usage. These are listed in the scope too, so there are no surprises.

How do you test before going live?

I build with test records and test questions first, and check normal cases, duplicates, missing data, failed actions and questions the AI should decline, against tests we agree in the scope. Then I hand over with notes on ownership, monitoring and recovery.

Where are you based, and who do you work with?

I'm based in Khairpur District, Sindh, Pakistan, and I work remotely with renovation, remodeling and home-service businesses anywhere in the world. Live calls are booked inside your business day, and everything is built inside your own accounts.

Are the projects on this site client work?

No. They are sandbox builds for Elite Home Renovations, a practice account in my LOOP Institute of Applied AI course, using test or demo data. Results are labelled as tested in a sample run, as build specs, or as estimates.

Where do your leads get stuck?

Tell me the manual process, the tools you already use and what a better result would look like. I'll reply with where an AI agent or an automation would help first, and where it wouldn't.
