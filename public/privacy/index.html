Privacy Policy

What personal data this website and my enquiry process use, why, and how you control it.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who I am

This website is run by Waqar Ahmed, an independent AI automation consultant (AI agents, GoHighLevel, n8n and websites) based in Khairpur District, Sindh, Pakistan, working remotely with clients worldwide ("I", "me"). For privacy questions, email meetwith.waqarahmed@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Enquiry details you choose to send, such as your name, work email, phone or WhatsApp number, trade type and the bottleneck you describe. The audit form does not store anything on this website: your answers are passed to Formspree, the form service that emails them to me.
  • Messages you send me by email, LinkedIn or other platforms.
  • Basic visit statistics from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why I use it (legal bases)

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal: steps taken at your request before a contract, or my legitimate interest in answering business enquiries.
  • To send occasional tips: only if you opted in, based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep the site secure and understand overall traffic: legitimate interest.

SMS and mobile messaging (A2P 10DLC)

If you give a mobile number and agree to be contacted, I use it only for the purpose you agreed to. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply.

To stop text messages, reply STOP to any message. You can also email meetwith.waqarahmed@gmail.com to opt out of any messages. Reply HELP for help.

Who receives data

I do not sell personal information. Data is processed by the services I use to run this site and my work: Cloudflare (hosting and analytics), Formspree (audit form delivery), Google (email), LinkedIn (if you message me there) and, for client projects, the client's own GoHighLevel and n8n accounts. When we work together, your business data stays in accounts you own.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and delete them on request unless I must keep them for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights (GDPR and UK GDPR)

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information I collect, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. I do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Contact and changes

To use any of these rights, email meetwith.waqarahmed@gmail.com. I may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
