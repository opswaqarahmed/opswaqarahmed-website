Website Estimate Assistant

A retrieval-augmented (RAG) AI agent with a chat widget on the Elite Home Renovations website. It answers visitor questions from the business's own content, day or night.

RAG agent + website chat widget. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Service name: I offer this build to clients as the 24/7 AI Website Receptionist. The case study below is the sandbox version, built for the Elite Home Renovations practice account.

The problem

Homeowners research renovation work in the evening and at weekends, when nobody is in the office. They want quick answers to simple questions: what the company does, where it works and how an estimate is arranged. If nobody answers, they can easily move on to the next contractor's website.

What I built

Why retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)

  • The business controls what the assistant knows: update the knowledge base, and the answers change.
  • Answers come from the company's own content, which keeps them specific to its services and process.
  • It is the foundation for an agent that can also capture enquiry details and hand them to the CRM.

How I would set it up for a live business

  • Build the knowledge base only from content the owner approves, and review it together.
  • Agree what the assistant must never do, such as quoting prices or promising dates, and what it says instead.
  • Test it with real customer questions, including ones it should decline, before it goes on the website.
  • Connect captured enquiries to GoHighLevel or n8n, so every conversation ends with an owner and a next step.

Before using this for a live business

  • Measure the current process first, so any improvement can be compared with a real baseline.
  • Agree which accounts, data and workflows the business owns. I work through a role-based team invite.
  • Confirm third-party costs, such as GoHighLevel, n8n and messaging plans.
  • Test normal, duplicate, missing-data and failure cases with the team's real rules.
