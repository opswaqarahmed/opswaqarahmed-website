One operating story: answer the demand, capture it, coordinate the work, and show the team what needs attention.

About this work. These are sandbox builds for Elite Home Renovations, a practice account in my LOOP Institute of Applied AI course, using test or demo data. They show how I build. They are not paid client deployments. Nothing here has been independently audited.

Featured case study

From site visit to supplier quote

When a job reaches the site-visit stage, n8n checks the project details, matches active suppliers by trade and city, sends individual quote requests, then records the result and a follow-up task in GoHighLevel.

  • The same job is never sent twice
  • Partial matches and failures are visible
  • CRM notes and dated follow-up tasks
