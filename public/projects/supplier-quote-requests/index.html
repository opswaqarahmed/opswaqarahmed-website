From site visit to supplier quote

An n8n workflow connected to GoHighLevel that matches active suppliers by trade and city, sends each one a quote request, and records the result and next follow-up in the CRM.

The problem

In renovation and home-service work, jobs get lost between enquiries, appointments, suppliers and follow-up. After a site visit, someone has to choose the right suppliers and send each one the project details. Suppliers get missed, and the customer's quote waits.

What I built

How it handles problems

  • Request cycles are duplicate-safe, so the same job is not sent twice.
  • Partial matches and send failures are visible instead of hidden.
  • CRM notes and dated follow-up tasks keep the team informed.

The estimate

Estimate, not a measured result: about 17 hours of manual work saved per month, assuming eight site visits each week.

A real business would need its own baseline first: how long supplier requests take today, and how many site visits it handles.

Before using this for a live business

  • Measure the current process first, so any improvement can be compared with a real baseline.
  • Agree which accounts, data and workflows the business owns. I work through a role-based team invite.
  • Confirm third-party costs, such as GoHighLevel, n8n and messaging plans.
  • Test normal, duplicate, missing-data and failure cases with the team's real rules.
