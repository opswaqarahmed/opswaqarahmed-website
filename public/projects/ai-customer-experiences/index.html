AI customer experiences

A 24/7 Conversation AI and Voice AI qualifier in GoHighLevel, plus a post-job reputation system that sends happy customers to Google and unhappy ones to the owner.

The problem

Homeowners ask questions at night and on weekends. After the job, a business needs more good reviews without unhappy customers being pushed to post publicly before the owner can help.

24/7 AI qualifier

Post-job reputation and review system

Review routing
RatingWhat happens
4–5 starsThe customer gets the Google Review link.
1–3 starsThe owner gets a private alert to follow up directly.

Asking one question at a time keeps the AI conversation simple for the customer, and the private alert gives the owner a chance to put things right.

Before using this for a live business

  • Measure the current process first, so any improvement can be compared with a real baseline.
  • Agree which accounts, data and workflows the business owns. I work through a role-based team invite.
  • Confirm third-party costs, such as GoHighLevel, n8n and messaging plans.
  • Test normal, duplicate, missing-data and failure cases with the team's real rules.
