Lead-to-site-visit system

A Free Estimate Request funnel, two pipelines, a 60-minute site-visit calendar and a reply-aware nurture sequence that stops as soon as a visit is booked.

  • Sandbox build

GoHighLevel. Last updated 14 November 2026.

  • Build spec2pipelines: Sales and Jobs
  • Build spec60 minsite-visit calendar slot
  • Build spec4nurture emails, Day 0 to Day 9
  • Build spec1Goal Event that stops the sequence

The problem

Estimate requests arrive at all hours. If nobody books the site visit quickly, the homeowner calls the next contractor. Follow-up emails also need to stop the moment a visit is booked.

What I built

The nurture sequence

Four-email sequence
EmailSent on
Email 1Day 0
Email 2Day 2
Email 3Day 5
Email 4Day 9

The sequence is reply-aware: when the homeowner books, the Goal Event stops further emails, so nobody is chased after they have already said yes.

Before using this for a live business

  • Measure the current process first, so any improvement can be compared with a real baseline.
  • Agree which accounts, data and workflows the business owns. I work through a role-based team invite.
  • Confirm third-party costs, such as GoHighLevel, n8n and messaging plans.
  • Test normal, duplicate, missing-data and failure cases with the team's real rules.
