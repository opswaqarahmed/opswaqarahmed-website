The problem

Most leads aren't lost. They stall between handoffs.

A renovation job passes through five handoffs before it becomes a quote. Each one is a place where a lead can sit with no answer, no owner and no next step.

Four handoffs where leads stall, and what the system does at each
HandoffWhere it stalls todayWhat the system does
Website visit to enquiryA visitor has a question at 10 pm. Nobody answers, so they leave and ask the next contractor.A 24/7 AI receptionist answers from your own content and captures the enquiry with the details your team needs.
Enquiry to first replyForm and chat enquiries wait until morning, or never get a reply.Every form, call and chat becomes a tagged contact in GoHighLevel, and the first reply goes out automatically.

How we work

Four steps. Scope agreed in writing first.

  1. Audit

    A 15-minute call. We trace one workflow and find where leads stall and where AI would actually help.

  2. Map and scope

    We agree the outcome, what is included, third-party and AI usage costs, and turnaround in writing before any build.

  3. Build and test

    Built inside your accounts with test records and test questions first: duplicates, missing data, failed actions and questions the AI should not answer.

  4. Hand over

    Documentation and a Loom walkthrough. You own every workflow, agent, knowledge base, login and record.
